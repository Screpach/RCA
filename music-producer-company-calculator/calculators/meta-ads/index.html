1. Audience saturation

A(r) = L × [1 − exp(−(r / s)^k)] Min: L=211,402.743; s=8.378526; k=0.862818 Max: L=248,646.342; s=8.372927; k=0.863133

Raw and isotonic curves remain visible. The fitted curve is used beyond 30 km.

2. Budget interpolation and extrapolation

Empirical(B) = piecewise-linear(active anchors) Planning(B) = 0.92 × Empirical(B) + 0.08 × WeightedPowerFit(B) At €50: 0.95 / 0.05 B > €100: F(B) = F(100) × (B / 100)^e

The €50 anchor remains in the data but receives weight 0.45 in the smooth trend; other budgets receive weight 1.00.

3. Long-duration fatigue

For D > 30: EffectiveExtraDays = 30/f × ln(1 + f × (D−30)/30) Total(D) = Total(30) + DailyRate30 × EffectiveExtraDays

f = 0.55 for impressions and 0.72 for visits. The function grows without a hard limit but with diminishing marginal delivery.

4. Frequency saturation

If FrequencyRaw ≤ 4: S = 1 Otherwise: S = 1 / [1 + ((FrequencyRaw−4)/11)^0.55] Planning impressions = Raw planning × S Planning visits = Raw planning × S^0.72

This keeps the empirical reproduction visible while producing a separate planning forecast for small audiences and long campaigns.

5. Demographic extrapolation

Audience = RadiusAudience × AgeMass / AgeMass(18–36) × GenderFactor × Breadth Gender factors: Women=1; Men=45.7/54.3; All=100/54.3

These coefficients are retained by request and remain extrapolations, not measured audience data.

6. Apple fee and Spanish IVA

iOS, media-spend input: PreTaxPayment = MediaSpend / 0.70 ServiceFee = PreTaxPayment − MediaSpend Consumer/no VAT ID: Cash IVA = PreTaxPayment × 21% Business with valid EU VAT ID: Cash IVA = 0 Accounting IVA = PreTaxPayment × 21%

Displayed service fee, cash IVA and total cash are rounded upward to whole euros.
